Cookies are small files stored on your device. We only use non-essential cookies if you agree to them, and you can change your mind at any time using .

Essential storage

We store your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask again on every page.

Analytics cookies (only with consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and whether appraisal requests are completed. These cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) are only set if you click Accept. With your consent, we also remember how you first arrived on the site so we know which channels bring enquiries.

Third-party services

If you choose to book a call, our scheduling provider Calendly loads in a window on this site and may set its own cookies. Booking a stay takes you to our direct-booking site, which has its own cookie policy.

Changing your choice

Use the cookie settings link in the footer of any page, or clear cookies in your browser settings.
