How it works

A straightforward path from first conversation to a home that's fully booked and beautifully looked after.

Request a Property AppraisalBook a 15-Minute Call

The four steps

  1. Request an appraisal

    Tell us about your home: where it is, its size and how it's let today. It takes a couple of minutes, and there's no obligation. Prefer to talk first? Book a 15-minute call.

  2. Talk it through

    We'll be in touch within 48 hours. We'll ask about your goals, how you use the home and what's working or not, and arrange to visit if it helps.

  3. Your proposal

    You'll receive a clear recommendation: which service fits, what your home could achieve, how we'd present it and what happens next.

  4. Launch and beyond

    We prepare the home, arrange photography, build the listing and set pricing. Then we look after guests, the property and reporting, every stay.

Common questions

How much does Staycay charge?

Our fees depend on the service you choose, the property and what's involved in looking after it, so we don't publish a one-size-fits-all rate. After your appraisal we'll give you a clear proposal with nothing hidden.

My home is already listed on Airbnb or Booking.com. Can you take it on?

Yes. Many owners come to us with an existing listing. We'll review how it's performing today and talk you through how we'd take it on.

How long does it take to get started?

It depends on the home and what's needed to get it ready. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your proposal.
