Performance questions

How do you set prices?

We price each night against local demand, seasonality, events and booking pace, and review strategy regularly rather than setting rates once a year.

Will you list my home on Airbnb and Booking.com?

We'll recommend the right mix of channels for your home, alongside Staycay's direct-booking site.

How will I know it's working?

You'll get clear reporting on bookings, revenue and how your home compares with the season before.
