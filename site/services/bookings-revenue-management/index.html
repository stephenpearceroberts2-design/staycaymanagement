Questions

Bookings & Revenue Management: your questions

What's the difference from Full Management?

With Bookings & Revenue Management you keep responsibility for housekeeping, maintenance and guests during their stay. We handle pricing, listings, distribution and bookings.

Can I switch to Full Management later?

Yes. Many owners start with one service and move to the other as their circumstances change.

Which booking channels do you use?

We'll recommend the right mix for your home during the appraisal, including Staycay's own direct-booking site.
