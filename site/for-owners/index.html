Questions

Owner questions, answered

How much does Staycay charge?

Our fees depend on the service you choose, the property and what's involved in looking after it, so we don't publish a one-size-fits-all rate. After your appraisal we'll give you a clear proposal with nothing hidden.

My home is already listed on Airbnb or Booking.com. Can you take it on?

Yes. Many owners come to us with an existing listing. We'll review how it's performing today and talk you through how we'd take it on.

Can I still use my home myself?

Of course. You can keep dates for your own stays, and we'll talk through how to plan them around the busiest periods.

Which areas do you cover?

We specialise in the Peak District, and also manage homes across Derbyshire and South Yorkshire. If you're just outside these areas, ask us anyway.

Do you take on homes that aren't holiday lets yet?

Yes. We can help you get a home ready to let, from presentation and photography to setting up listings and pricing.

Do you take every property?

No. We focus on high-quality homes we're confident we can do justice to, which is how we keep standards high for owners and guests.
