A slow weekend in the Hope Valley

4 November 20264 min readBy the Staycay team

The Hope Valley is the Peak District at its most generous: big views, easy-going villages and walks for every kind of weekend. Here's how we'd spend two slow days.

Saturday: Hathersage and Stanage Edge

Start with breakfast in Hathersage, then head up to Stanage Edge, the long gritstone escarpment above the village. The walk along the top is one of the finest in the Peak, with views across the valley in every direction.

Back in the village, Hathersage's heated open-air pool is a local favourite in the warmer months. In the evening, choose a pub with a fire and stay for dinner.

Sunday: Castleton and Ladybower

Drive the short way to Castleton for a morning on Mam Tor and the Great Ridge, or explore the village's show caves if the weather turns. In the afternoon, take a gentle walk around part of Ladybower Reservoir before heading home.

Where to stay

Several of our homes are in and around the Hope Valley, from cottages for two to barns for the whole family.
