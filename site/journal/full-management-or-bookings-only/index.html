Full management or bookings-only? Choosing the right support for your holiday home

28 October 20265 min readBy the Staycay team

There's no single right way to run a holiday home. The best set-up depends on where you live, how much time you have, and which parts of the job you actually enjoy.

When full management makes sense

  • You live some distance from the property, or abroad.
  • You don't want to be on call for guests, cleaners or contractors.
  • You want consistent standards without checking them yourself.
  • You see the home as an investment and want it run that way.

When bookings and revenue management is enough

  • You live nearby and enjoy looking after the home and welcoming guests.
  • You already have reliable housekeeping in place.
  • You want expert help with pricing, listings and filling the calendar.

Questions to ask yourself

How many hours a week do you spend on the property now? What happens when you're on holiday? Are you confident your nightly rates are right for every week of the year? Your answers usually make the choice clear.

If they don't, that's what an appraisal is for. We'll look at your home and how you'd like to run it, and recommend the service that fits.
