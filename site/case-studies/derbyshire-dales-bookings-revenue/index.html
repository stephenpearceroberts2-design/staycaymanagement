Filling the calendar: bookings and revenue support for a Derbyshire family home

+24%nights booked, year one
+17%average nightly rate
6 wksfrom appraisal to first booking

The challenge

The owner lives locally and takes real pride in housekeeping and guest welcome, but found pricing and channel management time-consuming and hard to get right.

Our approach

We rebuilt the listing, set up distribution across the right channels, and introduced dynamic pricing with minimum-stay rules tuned to the season. The owner kept the housekeeping and the welcome.

Before

  • Listed on one channel
  • Prices set once a year
  • Long gaps between stays

After

  • Distribution across the right channels
  • Pricing reviewed against local demand
  • Smarter minimum stays to close gaps
