Who we are

What we collect

  • Appraisal and contact forms: your name, email address, telephone number, property details and anything else you choose to tell us. We also record how you found the site (for example the page you landed on and any campaign tags in the link).
  • Call bookings: if you book a call, Calendly processes your name, email and chosen time on our behalf.

How and why we use it

We use enquiry details to respond to you and, where you're a property owner, to prepare and discuss an appraisal. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
