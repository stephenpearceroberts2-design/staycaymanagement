How it works

From first conversation to first booking

Our approach

  1. Request an appraisal

    Tell us about your home. It takes a couple of minutes, and there's no obligation.

  2. Talk it through

    We'll be in touch within 48 hours to understand your goals and, where it helps, visit the property.

  3. Your proposal

    A clear recommendation on the right service, what your home could achieve and how we'd present it.

  4. Launch and beyond

    We prepare, photograph and list your home, then look after everything from the first booking on.
