# Staycay website: content and photography guide

What Staycay needs to supply to replace the sample content. Copy can be sent as a Word doc or Google Doc per page; photos as full-size originals.

## Photography

Send **originals at full resolution** (JPEG or HEIC straight from the camera or photographer, not screenshots or WhatsApp copies). We create all web sizes and formats (WebP, 480 to 1600px wide) automatically.

| Use | Shape | Minimum size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Landscape 16:9 | 2400 × 1350 | Exterior at golden hour, space on the left for text |
| Page headers | Landscape 16:9 | 2400 × 1350 | One per main page; landscape, property or detail |
| Home card (Our Homes) | Landscape 4:3 | 1600 × 1200 | Best single image of each home |
| Property gallery | Mixed, mostly 4:3 | 1600 × 1200 | 5 to 12 per home: exterior, living, kitchen, bedrooms, bathroom, view, a detail |
| Split sections | Portrait 4:5 | 1200 × 1500 | Interiors and details |
| Team portraits | Portrait 4:5 | 1000 × 1250 | Same background and light for everyone |
| Case studies | Landscape 3:2 | 1600 × 1067 | Before and after pairs where possible |
| Journal | Landscape 3:2 | 1600 × 1067 | One lead image per article |

Cover the four themes: **Property, Place, People, Detail**. Keep the colour warm and natural, with no heavy filters. Name files by home and room (e.g. `millstone-barn-kitchen.jpg`), and include a one-line description of each, which becomes the alt text.

## Copy, page by page

Write in plain, warm British English. Lead with the benefit to the reader. Don't include management fee percentages. Any figure (revenue, occupancy, ratings) must be verifiable.

**For each page, send:** the H1, a one-sentence intro, section headings (H2) with their copy, any FAQs (question and a 40–80 word answer), and a suggested page title (≤ 60 characters) and meta description (≤ 155 characters). We'll check the heading structure and internal links when we put it in.

**For each case study:** the home and location, service, the challenge, what Staycay changed, before/after points, 2–3 headline results with the period they cover, an owner quote with permission to use it, and photos.

**For each home:** name, village, sleeps/bedrooms/bathrooms, dog policy, 2–3 paragraphs of description, 6–10 features, a short local-area paragraph, categories (couples, families, large groups, dog friendly, walkers), and the booking URL on the direct-booking site.
